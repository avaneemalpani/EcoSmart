📦 Your e-waste packing checklist

Your pickup is scheduled. Please prepare your items safely before the collection agent arrives.

  • Small devices: Place phones, tablets, chargers and accessories in a sturdy cardboard box; cushion them so they do not move around.
  • Batteries: Never puncture, crush, bend, open or burn batteries. Cover exposed terminals with non-conductive tape and keep batteries separate from metal objects.
  • Swollen, leaking or hot batteries: Do not pack or transport them in a normal bag or box. Keep people away and contact EcoSmart support for specialist guidance.
  • Broken screens / sharp parts: Avoid direct handling; use a rigid container and protective material without pressing on the damaged area.
  • Keep devices dry: Keep electronics away from water and other liquids. Use a leakproof outer container only for items that are safe to handle; do not seal a damaged battery in an airtight container.
  • Protect your data: Back up important files, sign out of accounts, remove SIM/memory cards, and factory-reset devices where appropriate.
  • Keep items accessible: Label the box with your pickup tracking ID and keep it ready at the agreed collection point.
⚠️ Do not place e-waste or batteries in regular household rubbish. The collection team will confirm any special handling needed.
