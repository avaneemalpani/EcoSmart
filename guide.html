E-Waste Disposal Guide

Simple steps to prepare electronics safely and protect your personal information.

📦 Pack items safely

  • Use a sturdy cardboard box for small devices and cushion items to prevent movement.
  • Keep devices dry and separate loose batteries from electronics and metal objects.
  • Never puncture, crush, bend, open or burn batteries.
  • Cover exposed battery terminals with non-conductive tape.
  • Do not handle or transport swollen, leaking, smoking or hot batteries yourself. Keep people away and seek specialist guidance.
  • For broken screens or sharp parts, avoid direct contact and use a rigid container.
  • Do not put electronics or batteries in household rubbish.

🔐 Data privacy checklist

  • Back up photos, documents and other important files.
  • Sign out of email, cloud and device accounts.
  • Remove SIM cards, memory cards and accessories containing personal data.
  • Use the device manufacturer's reset/secure erase process where appropriate.
  • For business devices or sensitive data, seek verified professional data destruction.
  • Do not upload identity documents or unrelated personal information to your pickup request.

♻️ What can I submit?

Laptops/desktops, mobile phones, tablets, chargers/adapters, batteries and other electronic items can be selected in the pickup form. Final acceptance may depend on condition and safe-handling requirements.

❓ Help centre

When are EcoPoints credited?

EcoPoints are credited after the recycling status is confirmed by the operations team.

Can I cancel or change a pickup?

Pending requests can be cancelled or rescheduled from your dashboard. Contact support if the pickup has already been assigned.

What if a battery is swollen or leaking?

Do not pack it in a regular bag or box or transport it yourself. Keep people away and contact a qualified specialist for safe handling.

What happens to my photos?

Photos are attached to your pickup request for item verification. Upload only relevant device photos.
