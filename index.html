Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

What can I recycle?

EcoSmart currently supports laptops/desktops, mobile phones, tablets, chargers/adapters, batteries and other e-waste categories.

When do I receive EcoPoints?

Points are credited only after an admin confirms that the pickup has been collected and processed responsibly.

Can I see my pickup status?

Yes. Your dashboard shows the request timeline, tracking ID, assigned agent and collection status.

What happens to my uploaded photos?

Photos are stored with your pickup request so the operations team can verify the item. Avoid uploading unrelated personal documents.
